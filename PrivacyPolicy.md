# Bible CUV 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026年11月 · Last updated: November 2026

## 中文

Bible CUV（圣经和合本）不收集、不储存、也不传送任何个人资料。

- 本应用无需注册或登录，不使用任何账户。
- 本应用不连接网络，不含广告、统计或第三方追踪工具。
- 您的阅读记录、收藏和设定（字号、背景）只保存在您的设备上，不会上传。
- 删除本应用即会删除这些资料。
- 您导出的文本或PDF文件，只会分享到您自己选择的地方。

如有疑问，请联系：davidlin_aus@yahoo.com

## English

Bible CUV does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- No sign-up, login, or account is needed.
- The app does not use the internet and contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking.
- Your reading history, bookmarks, and settings (text size, background) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
- Deleting the app deletes this data.
- Text or PDF files you export are shared only where you choose to send them.
